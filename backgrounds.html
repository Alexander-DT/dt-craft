Component lab · 06  /  Backgrounds

One page, six rooms.

Most sites have one background and light it differently. This is the other approach: a sequence of grounds a reader travels between, where each one arrives over the last in its own way — covered, wiped, opened through, folded up or pushed sideways. Every transition is scrubbed from the scrollbar, so all six run backwards as cleanly as forwards.

Ground 01 · Aurora

Light with no edges.

Five ribbons bent across the frame on offset loops, rendered at 55% of device resolution and blurred — because an aurora has no detail to lose, and the blur is the whole effect. It screens onto the dark ground and multiplies onto paper, so neither theme washes it out.

  • Ribbons 5
  • Render scale 0.55×
  • Canvas blur
  • Blend flips with theme
Ground 02 · Terrain

A landscape, running at you.

Rows spaced by one-over-z rather than evenly, which is the entire difference between perspective and a stack of wavy lines. The height field is three summed sines, so the ridges never repeat inside a viewing.

  • Rows 24
  • Perspective divide
  • Horizon at 34%
  • Continuous scroll

Arrived by cover

Ground 03 · Plasma

Interference, warped.

Four sine fields sampled through a domain warp, computed at a ninth of the frame and scaled back up. That turns a full-resolution pixel loop into a twenty-thousand pixel one — a shader's look without a shader's budget.

  • Per-pixel
  • Divisor 9
  • Domain warp
  • ~20k px / frame

Arrived by curtain

Ground 04 · Circuit

Traces, and something travelling them.

Manhattan paths snapped to a grid, each terminating in a pad, each carrying a pulse that walks the segments at a constant speed rather than a constant parameter — so a long leg does not go faster than a short one.

  • Traces 26
  • Arc-length pulse
  • Grid-snapped
  • Radial glow

Arrived by iris

Ground 05 · Caustics

The same maths, a harder curve.

Identical machinery to the plasma, with the transfer curve raised to the seventh power. That is all it takes to turn broad interference into the thin bright filaments light makes on the floor of a pool.

  • Shared engine
  • Divisor 7
  • Transfer x⁷
  • Filament falloff

Arrived by fold

Ground 06 · Orbits

Paths, and the things on them.

Eight rotated ellipses with a body on each. The trail is an arc of the same ellipse rather than a fading line, so it sits exactly on the path instead of near it — which is the detail that stops it reading as a screensaver.

  • Orbits 8
  • Rotated ellipses
  • Trail on-path
  • Both directions

Arrived by slide

At section scale

Three grounds, three rates.

Not every background needs a whole screen. The same fields stack inside one band and travel at different rates against the scroll, which buys depth without taking the page hostage.

  • Three layers
  • -6% / -18% / -34%
  • One scrubbed value
  • Section height, not viewport

Depth is a rate, not a shadow.

Three fields at three speeds read as three distances. Nothing here is blurred and nothing is scaled — only the rate differs.

The set

Seventeen grounds.

Every field the lab can draw, at thumbnail size. One engine; a canvas declares which field it is and how hard to run it. Each reads its tint from its own host element, so a field inside an inverted island keeps the bright accent while the page around it flips.

Every field sleeps when it leaves the screen and when the tab hides. Inside the scene rig above, only the two scenes actually in play are drawn — the other four hold their last frame behind visibility: hidden. Under prefers-reduced-motion each paints a single static frame and the rig becomes six ordinary sections.
