Component lab · 03  /  Testimonials

The section everybody skips.

A row of three cards with five stars each is wallpaper. These five treat a quote as something with a place, a weight and an order — so a reader has a reason to stop at one rather than scroll past all of them.

Four of the quotes below are the real ones from the live site. The rest are marked Specimen — placeholder copy standing in for volume, not attributed to anyone.

Testimonials 01 · Orbit

The one at the top is the one you read.

The clients circle a fixed centre. Selecting any of them rotates the whole ring to bring that one to the top rather than swapping the quote underneath — so the reader can see where they are in the set at all times.

  • Ring rotates, avatars counter-rotate
  • Second ring, slow
  • Arrow keys
  • Auto-advance until touched
Seven on the ring · step 51.4° Settle 1.05s, overdamped — nothing bounces

Testimonials 02 · Leaning wall

Volume, without a list.

Three columns leaning back into the page, running against each other so the wall never reads as one block sliding. Hover anywhere and the whole thing stops; hover a card and it steps thirty-eight pixels towards you.

  • 3D lean
  • Counter-running columns
  • Pause on hover
  • Card lifts on Z

Testimonials 03 · Spotlight rail

One at full size.

The quiet one, and the one that reads best on a phone. A single quote at display size with the roster laid out as a filmstrip beneath it — the strip scrolls itself so the active name stays in view.

  • Tablist semantics
  • Roving tabindex
  • Strip auto-centres
  • Figures per client

Testimonials 04 · Coverflow

Reading position as a place.

Cards to either side turn away from the reader and dim with distance, so there is one obvious place to be looking and a visible sense of how much is left in either direction.

  • Turn ±46°
  • Depth by distance
  • Drag steps
  • Focus recentres

Testimonials 05 · Ledger

Ruled, numbered, entered.

The plainest of the five and the one that survives a print stylesheet. A row opens in place using grid-template-rows: 0fr → 1fr, so it opens to its real height without anybody measuring it first.

  • 0fr → 1fr
  • No measured heights
  • One open at a time
  • aria-expanded
