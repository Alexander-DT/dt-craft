RTOPilot

RTOPilot is an all-in-one training management system that makes operations easy for education and training providers.

IndustryEducation, SaaS
Year2024
ToolsWebflow, Figma, Wized
Work typeWebsite design, SEO, ongoing care

The brief

Software that had to look as simple as it is.

01 · What they needed

A brand that argued against its own category.

RTOPilot started with a simple idea — education and training software doesn't have to be complicated or expensive. They needed a website and brand that reflects this, while demonstrating how high-performing their product is compared to the cumbersome, legacy software offered by their competitors.

02 · What we did

A fast Webflow build, pointed at sign-ups.

We delivered a fast, responsive Webflow website that highlights RTOPilot's easy, user-friendly nature. Clear calls-to-action drive sign-ups and demo bookings, while a dynamic feature encourages users to migrate from competitors.

03 · Search

Built to rank from day one.

Our SEO strategy included keyword optimisation, meta tag improvements and structured content, ensuring RTOPilot ranks well from launch rather than waiting months to be found.

04 · Since launch

Still shipping.

The website achieved strong search ranking performance immediately, generating leads for the business. We provide ongoing SEO and web maintenance, helping RTOPilot grow into what it set out to be.
