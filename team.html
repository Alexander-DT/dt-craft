Team 01 · Orbit

Everybody, and one of them.

Two rings, the outer one dashed and slow. Choosing a role rotates the ring to bring that plate to the top and fills the centre — so the team is never reduced to the one person you happen to be reading about.

  • Ring rotates, plates counter-rotate
  • Centre follows selection
  • Arrow keys
  • Auto-advance until touched
Eight on the ring · step 45° Outer ring 90s, continuous

Team 02 · Roster

The portrait rides the pointer.

A ruled list that stays a list. The portrait is a single fixed-position element outside the scroll rig, lerped towards the cursor — one node moving rather than eight images fading. It is off entirely on touch, where there is no pointer to follow.

  • One shared portrait node
  • Outside the scroll transform
  • Lerped follow
  • Off on touch and reduced motion

Team 03 · Discipline helix

The roster as an object.

Angle from the index, height from the same index, so the column reads as one continuous thread rather than a stack of plates. The whole screw turns three hundred degrees across the section — driven by the same scrub the rest of the lab uses.

  • Two turns
  • Radius from container width
  • Scroll-scrubbed rotation
  • Fixed angle under reduced motion
